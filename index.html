Our Story

A family table in Al-Hawari

Flaminio is a family-run Italian restaurant in Al-Hawari, Benghazi. Our kitchen works with imported Italian ingredients, pasta made by hand each morning and a wood-fired oven that never quite cools down. What we care about most is simple: warm hospitality and food we would happily serve our own family.
